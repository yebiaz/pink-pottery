Where your money goes

Every yuan raised goes directly to patients in the Breast Cancer Ward at Kunshan No. 1 Hospital (昆山市第一人民医院), right here in our own city.

Treatment for breast cancer is long, and the cost of medication, travel and time away from work adds up fast for families. Your bid is a small, local way to help patients and families at our hospital.

Pink Week

This showcase is the finale of Breast Cancer Awareness Week. On Day 4, Pink Pottery, students came to the Performance Cafe to make their own pink pottery and clay ribbons. Many chose to donate what they made so it could raise money for patients.

Medic Mindset

The Medic Mindset is a student-led organization for students interested in medicine and health. Through events like Pink Week, we try to bring health awareness out of the classroom and turn it into something our campus can do together.

Ceramics Club

The Ceramics Club taught every Pink Pottery session, worked with the Kunshan studio, and fired and glazed every piece on this page. Thank you for sharing your clay, kilns and patience!

Early detection matters

Get to know what's normal for your body, and talk to a doctor if you notice a new lump, a change in shape or skin, or nipple changes. Encourage the women in your life to keep up with screenings.

How the silent donation works

  1. Pick a piece. Browse the ribbons and larger pieces. Each card shows the current top bid.
  2. Bid with your NetID. Enter your NetID and an amount higher than the current top bid. Whole yuan only.
  3. Get outbid? Bid again. Each evening we email netid@duke.edu with where your bids stand.
  4. Bidding closes Sunday Nov 29 at midnight (China time). Bids placed online and at the booth count the same.
  5. Monday morning, every winner gets an email with how to pay and when to pick up their piece.

Fine print

  • A bid is a pledge to donate that amount if you win, so please only bid what you're happy to give.
  • Only the first two letters of your NetID are shown publicly (e.g. ab•••).
  • Bids under someone else's NetID will be removed. If a winner doesn't pay, the piece goes to the next-highest bidder.
  • Every piece is handmade by a student, so small wobbles and glaze drips are part of the charm.
